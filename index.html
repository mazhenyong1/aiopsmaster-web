The incident pipeline

Six steps your SRE does by hand — automated end to end.

Every alert flows through the same disciplined path. You stay in control of the gates; the platform does the toil.

📥

Ingest

Alerts from any monitor land in one console, normalized and deduplicated.

🧭

Govern

Per-group, per-alert routing decides what gets diagnosed, by which engine.

🧠

Diagnose

An LLM reads runbooks, gathers evidence, and writes the root cause.

⚡

Act

Matched playbooks run the fix through audited, gated, reversible steps.

📣

Report

A clean RCA card lands in chat — verdict, evidence, and what was done.

♻️

Learn

Every incident sharpens the runbooks, the routing, and the next fix.

One console for every alert — signal, not noise.

  • →Blast-radius ranking. Severity reflects impact, not just threshold.

02 · AI root-cause analysis

A written RCA, with the evidence — not a guess.

For each incident the engine reads the relevant runbooks, opens the dashboards and traces an engineer would, captures the evidence, and writes a verdict you can act on or audit.

  • →Runbook-grounded. The verdict cites the exact SOP step it followed, never a hallucinated command.
  • →Evidence attached. Screenshots, metrics and log excerpts travel with the card.
  • →Honest on misses. When it isn't covered, it says so and asks — it never bluffs a fix.

03 · Per-alert governance

Control it down to the single alert type.

A group default sets the baseline; override any individual alert type without touching the rest. Noisy item? Silence just that one. Known pattern? Pin it to a specific playbook.

  • →Inherit by default. Configure the two exceptions, not the forty alert types.
  • →Route by runbook. Playbooks auto-match the alert — you don't hand-pick per group.
  • →Audited & reversible. Every toggle is logged; one click returns to default.

04 · Playbooks that follow the SOP

Your runbooks, turned into reviewed, executable steps.

A playbook declares what it triggers on and the exact steps to run — read-only evidence gathering by default, write actions only behind approval gates. The branches mirror your real SOP.

  • →Trigger by signature. Title and body patterns match the alert type precisely — a 5xx never lands in the 4xx playbook.
  • →Gated writes. Three gates — platform-authored only, idempotent, fully audited — before anything changes.
  • →Verify in-loop. After the fix it re-checks the real state before declaring success.

05 · Living knowledge base

Runbooks that ingest, link and stay fresh on their own.

Point it at a doc URL and the SOP is pulled in, cleaned, indexed and linked to the alert types it covers. When an incident uses a runbook that isn't in the library yet, it's ingested on the spot.

  • →Auto-ingest on use. Find a runbook mid-incident → it's in the library, no manual import.
  • →Coverage map. See exactly which alert types still have no SOP — ranked by how often they fire.
  • →Always in sync. Source doc changes → the indexed copy refreshes.

06 · Agentic evidence capture

It opens the dashboards an engineer would — and screenshots the proof.

A managed fleet of headless browsers navigates your internal tools read-only: following SOP links, reading diagnostic pages, and capturing the evidence that makes an RCA defensible — all without a human driving a mouse.

  • →Read-only by design. It looks and captures; write actions stay behind playbook gates.
  • →Self-healing sessions. Dropped logins recover automatically, pool-wide, in minutes.
  • →On-call aware. Routes the summary to whoever's on duty, in their timezone.
